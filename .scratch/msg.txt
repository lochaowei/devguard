feat: check Java cyclomatic complexity and banned APIs with PMD, hint at retired Checkstyle declarations (#193)

The PMD ruleset generator and check now carry complexity.cyclomatic
(CyclomaticComplexity methodReportLevel) and deps.bannedApis (a generated
BannedApis XPath rule over imports), which only Checkstyle enforced before.

tools.checkstyle, tools.maven-checkstyle-plugin and the Checkstyle-era values
of lint.tool / lint.rules in an existing .devguard.yaml now get a
declaration/retired-item hint that says Checkstyle was removed, not an error.
